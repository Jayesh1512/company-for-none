## Product Purpose

**Let an owner define a company once, then supervise its results and exceptions instead of assigning each routine task.** The intended loop is brief/objective → manager and permanent backlog → scoped specialist/executive or developer → JBrain-backed work and delivery → observed gap → deduplicated backlog → proposed improvement → fixed baseline/candidate evaluation → scoped version promotion → future tasks use the current effective head. Rollback preserves history.

Agent-managed operation is the default for new companies. Owner-managed operation keeps owner-managed team assembly and work. “None” means bounded agent operation; the owner remains responsible for policy, budget, client authority and exceptional decisions. Neither mode selection nor a saved role gives an agent arbitrary permission.

The [current product guide](docs/COMPANYOS_GUIDE.md) explains the day-to-day flow, roles, healing, upgrades, buying and owner actions. Its [capability/status table](docs/COMPANYOS_GUIDE.md#current-capability-and-status) is the concise current boundary. Historical plans remain review records.

## Product Principles

- Lead with the objective, responsible agent, result and next action.
- Let routine work proceed within preapproved scope; ask the owner for specific exceptions.
- Make real persistence, active versions and source citations visible.
- Separate requests, drafts and observations from purchases, sales and verified outcomes.
- Keep controlled demonstrations explicit and current financial/knowledge authority in Rules.
- Carry a tested repair into actual future context and keep rollback available.
