## Remaining exact blockers

1. **Supported durable encrypted-output proof:** current official SDK0.3.0 and inspected main expose a response-decryption closure, with no supported export/import of response key state. Saved ciphertext/signature or a success boolean cannot establish replayable original plaintext after restart. Upstream supported recovery APIs and protected key/collateral storage are needed under the existing security model. A different route/guarantee requires an explicit security choice and separate implementation/evidence; none was silently selected.
2. **Live authority and provider identity:** existing protected Cloud/Agent credentials, verified organization/key/credit attribution, request-specific technical cost bounds and exact work authority are absent. USD5 is a ceiling, not those facts. Balance snapshots publish no provider watermark and cannot initialize credit by themselves.
3. **Checkout/remedy/movement:** exact spendable route/account/network, independently adequate settlement/finality evidence and an executable funded principal+fee refund route remain unproven. A signed quote, provider SUCCESS or transparency receipt is not silently promoted to chain-final money.
4. **Acquisition:** no verified autonomous Cloud cash-to-credit purchase route or actual paid skill-license supplier/delivery contract exists in the inspected integration. Staking, user sessions and task-marketplace output are not substitutes for an authorized purchase/license.
5. **Specialized tools:** missing response proof, undocumented overhead or aggregate service-round bounds and deprecated reporting migration prevent claiming all eleven tools are live-enabled.
